Spatial intelligence for the factory

Know exactly where every camera is.

Camera localization turns images and video into spatially anchored manufacturing data. By estimating a camera's precise 6DoF pose inside an aircraft, tooling cell, hangar or digital twin, vision systems can measure, inspect, guide and verify work in the correct physical context.

Primary value levers

Camera localization improves the reliability and scalability of downstream computer-vision applications because observations are no longer isolated 2D pixels—they become spatially registered manufacturing events.

Why localization matters

Many vision applications can detect objects. Localization answers the next business-critical question: where is that observation on the aircraft?
